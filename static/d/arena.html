Magic: The Gathering, set by set — one expansion per sphere, every card curated by its basic worth. Live market prices from Scryfall (the non-foil USD it mirrors from TCGplayer), bucketed into worth tiers: chase, staples, role-players, bulk. Give the domain a set, it becomes a sphere — the whole list on the table, sorted by what each card is actually worth. The blade in the medallion is the set's edge: what's sharp, what's bulk.
