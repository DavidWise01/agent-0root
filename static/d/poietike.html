POIĒTIKĒ (ποιητική — Aristotle's word for the POETIC ART, the systematic anatomy of how a made thing, a narrative, is put together) — the domain of NARRATIVE STRUCTURE ITSELF: not the myths (those are MYTHOS) nor the book-worlds (BIBLÍON), but the GRAMMAR beneath them. The POSI-CORP thread builds it as a coordinate system: a WITHIN-WORK column (theme 1.0 → beat 0.1) crossed with a BETWEEN-WORK lineage (old → new, links of measurable tightness), governed above by archetype-pool / medium / history and bounded [atom … perceiver]. Genre is treated as an ALGEBRA — a genre is a PLACE (noun), a tone-wrapper a STANCE (adverb), and they compose into named subgenres; noir is not a sixth genre but an OPERATOR that runs over the others.
