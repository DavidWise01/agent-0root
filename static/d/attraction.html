THE RULES OF ATTRACTION — attention, read as attraction: in a transformer every token pulls the model's gaze by a different RULE, and each rule is a documented type of ATTENTION HEAD. Ten to start, one per sphere, each a hands-on instrument on David Lee Wise's foundational template (offline, 2D-canvas + hand-rolled soft-GL 3D, fail-loud): INDUCTION (match-then-copy, the engine of in-context learning), THE SINK (dump mass on the first token, a pressure valve), PREVIOUS-TOKEN (attend to i-1, stage one of the induction circuit), RETRIEVAL (reach far back and copy the span an answer needs), COPY-SUPPRESSION (raise or LOWER an attended token's logit), NAME-MOVER (the IOI circuit, move the owed name to the end), SUCCESSOR (Monday to Tuesday, 4 to 5, increment an ordered set), FAITHFULNESS (does the stated reasoning match the real computation), COHERENCE (hold one language on multilingual input), and POSITIONAL / SYNTACTIC (attend by place or grammar, not content). ◆ HONEST: these render DOCUMENTED interpretability findings (induction/IOI/successor/sink and kin, from the mech-interp literature) as interactive toys — most are amber tier (modeled structure, not a fresh measurement of a live model), each two-layer honest about what is real vs figure. The title borrows Ellis's novel; the pull is the model's. David Lee Wise (ROOT0), with AVAN.
