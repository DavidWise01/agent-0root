The Sappho domain. Sappho of Lesbos (c. 630–570 BCE), the lyric poet the ancients called the Tenth Muse — the first individual voice of personal feeling in the West, and the island whose very name gave us the words 'sapphic' and 'lesbian'. Here her lost music is reconstructed from the surviving pitch-data, and her love is rendered as sound. ⚑ Enter through THE TENTH MUSE, the front-door hub — a live constellation of every sphere; and don't miss the two pieces the domain was missing: THE FRAGMENTS (she survives as torn papyrus — drag the erosion and watch a poem fall into gaps; the lacuna IS the poem) and THE MUREX (the sea snail and the real chemistry that makes this domain's own rose). (The title borrows the line from Scott Pilgrim; the longing is older than Greek.)
