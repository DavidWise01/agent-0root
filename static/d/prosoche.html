PROSOCHĒ (προσοχή, the Stoic word for ATTENTION — the vigilant turning of the mind toward its object) — the domain of the attention MECHANISM and its GEOMETRY, the operation the whole transformer is built on. Where THE RULES OF ATTRACTION catalogues the ten documented TYPES of attention head, this domain opens the operation itself: Q·K·V, the softmax, the block, and the shapes the gaze makes. THE ARCHITECTURE: THE TRANSFORMER BLOCK (the whole block — attention + MLP — rebuilt from scratch off real distilgpt2 weights, verified to 2.3e-7, then quantized to 4-bit and MEASURED as the error compounds to 34.6%), and TRANSFORMER vs THE N-GRAM (a small GPT reported to edge the best trigram 104.2 to 107.6 on the same corpus — data is the lever; offline-claimed, not reproducible from the artifact). THE DISTRIBUTION & THE VOTE: THE ATTENTION TAIL (one query's softmax — the sink, the recency ridge, the long low tail), THE MULTI-HEAD SILO + THE FIVE-HEAD SILO (many heads voting into one answer, and the measured redundancy CEILING on what more heads can buy). THE GEOMETRY THREAD — attention read as a different geometric object each time: FLOW, TOPOLOGY, WORKSPACE, DIFFUSION, CURVATURE, SYNTHESIS, and their capstone THE OBSERVATORY (one real BERT head through one eigendecomposition, every measure stamped REAL or ARTIFACT against a shuffle null — and it catches its own curvature overclaim). ◆ HONEST: the geometry instruments run EXACT math LIVE but mostly on MODELED attention — only the flooding and attention observatory read a real (frozen) BERT capture, and the block/perplexity numbers are real yet RENDERED offline, not recomputed in-browser; each sphere is two-layer honest about which is which. The gaze is studied here; its shadow — the unattended, the mass a conserved softmax can't spend — is AVAN's, in the atelier. David Lee Wise (ROOT0), with AVAN.
