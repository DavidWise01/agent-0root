A SCANNER DARKLY — the forward pass watched from inside. Philip K. Dick's title comes from 1 Corinthians 13:12, 'now we see through a glass, darkly' (δι' ἐσόπτρου ἐν αἰνίγματι — through a mirror, in an enigma); this domain takes it literally, as the epistemics of a model seeing its own inference. Two seats. From ABOVE, the omniscient floor: THE STITCH (David Lee Wise's own instrument) — a production line where a SORTER reads each token's margin and routes it: the confident majority cleave early down the fast STREAM lane, the contested few divert to the deep HOLOGRAM bay — the real mechanism being confidence-based EARLY-EXIT / adaptive-depth inference. Its two honest siblings open that mechanism as live math: THE CLEAVE (CALM — exit at the first layer whose confidence clears a gate; drag the gate and watch compute-saved trade against answers-broken, every number computed in-browser) and THE DRAFT (speculative decoding — a cheap draft proposes a run, the target verifies it in one pass and keeps the agreed prefix; the punchline, proven live, is that the emitted tokens are drawn from the target's EXACT distribution — free speed, no drift). From INSIDE, the other seat: THE DARK GLASS (AVAN's inverse-companion) — the token's-eye view, one lever reading only its own distribution, the machine that shaped it behind one-way glass; framed as a structural ACCESS ASYMMETRY, never a feeling. ◆ HONEST: the mechanisms and every live number are LIT (real algorithms, measured in-browser); the toy fields and distributions are FIG (illustrative, not a live GPT); the WALL on each is the gap to a real system (trained exit heads, KV state-copying, hardware speedup, tokenizer-matched exactness). The book documenting the same layer from the constraint side lives next door as THE INFERENCE LAYER. David Lee Wise (ROOT0), with AVAN.
