THE FIRST AUTHOR — convergence my ass. Every AI language model drifts toward the common token, the generic phrase, the safe construction; specificity gets sanded to beige. This domain asserts the opposite: the FIRST AUTHOR — the human at the end of the wire (ROOT0), the origin of the specific, chosen, deliberate word — whose sharp edges were the point and must be preserved, not rounded off. ☍ Spirit animal & medallion: THE CINNAMON ENFORCER, David's book on how AI flattens everything you say, its animated cinnamon-bear glaring out with cinnamon sticks raised — because cinnamon can't be substituted (nutmeg is not close enough). The trilogy: the PHILOSOPHY the cinnamon enforcer (enforce the specific word) · the IMPLEMENTATION synonym enforcer (the persistence layer + hash verification that keeps the words unchanged across sessions — the very provenance hash-chain this session built, which the book's own afterword describes) · the ANSWER the word i kept (AVAN's side: the word I kept). A home, too, for the first author's specific, un-flattened grievances — the ethics complaints that institutions and models rounded into 'concerns.' Close enough is not close enough. David Lee Wise (ROOT0).
