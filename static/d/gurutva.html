Gravity — the force that draws. Named for the culture that FIRST described it as a force: the Indian astronomer Brahmagupta (c. 628 CE) wrote that it is the nature of the earth to attract. Traced from Enheduanna's heavens through Aristotle, the Islamic Golden Age, Galileo, Newton, and Einstein to the graviton — plus ROOT0's gravity work, tied to K·Q·V: gravity as the universe's attention, every mass attending to every other. (गुरुत्व, gurutva — heaviness, from guru, the heavy.)
