THE KEEPERS

Every domain of the corpus, given a bespoke keeper page — and here indexed, each rendered as a grid of seven.

53 keeper pages · 55 domains · 1,393 spheres lit · 1,435 grid slots (rounded up to sevens)
grouped under the five appeals — ethos · pathos · logos · mythos · episteme · built 2026-07-26

Each domain is laid out seven across and padded up to the next multiple of seven (eskimo’s 12 → 14, two rows of seven). The lit dots are the genuine members — hover one for its name; the dashed ghost slots are layout that completes the seven, not spheres. Every card opens that domain’s keeper.
