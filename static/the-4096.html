Apply -+ and the tower doubles. The first 2048 is DAVID (i) — the built, sealed, node-verified corpus. The second is its strict inverse: SHADOW (−i), each leaf the complement of its twin — the mirror the corpus's own law (PATRICIA ⟂ TOPH, −i to i) already implies. Two cathedrals, conjugate, crowned by one hash.

TOWER 2 is the strict inversion — each of its 2048 leaves is the bitwise complement of the corresponding sealed leaf (inverting twice returns the original, verified), folded to its own real root₂.
