1 WHAT IT IS · WHAT IT DOES · FACT OR FICTION
A real number is normal if every digit, every pair, every block of every length appears with exactly its fair frequency. Borel proved (1909) that almost every real number is normal — pick one at random and normality is certain. Then try to NAME one: π? Unproven. e? Unproven. √2, ln 2? Unproven, all of them — a century of silence. The only certified specimens are artificial: Champernowne’s 0.123456789101112… (1933), normal by construction. Almost everything has the property; almost nothing can be shown to.
