THE MIQUEL

four circles meeting at one point
1 WHAT IT IS · WHAT IT DOES · FACT OR FICTION
Miquel’s theorem (the pivot theorem) is a small miracle of circle geometry. Take any triangle ABC and pick one point on each side — P on BC, Q on CA, R on AB. Draw the three circles through a vertex and its two neighbouring chosen points: circle (AQR), circle (BRP), circle (CPQ). Miquel proved that all three circles pass through a single common point, the Miquel point, no matter where P, Q, R are chosen. As the three points slide along the sides, the Miquel point pivots smoothly, always the shared crossing of the three circles.

LIT verified live: for tens of thousands of random triangles and random points on the sides, the second intersection of circles (AQR) and (BRP) lies on circle (CPQ) as well — the three circles concur (window.__miquel).
