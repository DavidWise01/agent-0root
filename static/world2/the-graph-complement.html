THE GRAPH COMPLEMENT

flip every edge, flip again, home
1 WHAT IT IS · WHAT IT DOES · FACT OR FICTION
The graph complement flips every relationship: in the complement Ĝ of a graph G, two vertices are joined exactly when they are not joined in G. Friendship becomes strangerhood and back. Together G and Ĝ partition the complete graph, so their edge counts sum to C(n,2); many properties dualize (an independent set in G is a clique in Ĝ). And it is an involution: complementing twice restores the original graph. A graph that is isomorphic to its own complement is self-complementary — like the 5-cycle C₅, whose complement is again a 5-cycle.

LIT verified live: over 20,000 random graphs, complementing twice returns the original (a true involution) and e(G)+e(Ĝ) = C(n,2); and C₅ is shown self-complementary — its complement is 2-regular with 5 edges (window.__graph_complement).
