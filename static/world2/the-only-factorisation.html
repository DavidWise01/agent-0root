1 WHAT IT IS · WHAT IT DOES · FACT OR FICTION
A drawing shows four arms around a centre. Asked whether the picture and the arithmetic agree, the answer turned out stronger than agreement: among equal arm sizes, exactly one reaches the total. 84 = 4096. The four-around-one figure is not a diagram over the number — it is the number’s only four-way equal factorisation.

LIT verified live and then exhaustively. Of the arm sizes 2, 4, 8, 16 and 32, only 8 lands on 4096. Searching every integer arm size from 2 to 4096 returns the same single solution. Four arms of three bits is 12 bits, and 212 is the total.
