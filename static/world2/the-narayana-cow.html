THE NARAYANA COW

a sequence at the supergolden ratio
1 WHAT IT IS · WHAT IT DOES · FACT OR FICTION
Narayana’s cows sequence comes from a 14th-century puzzle: a cow produces one calf a year, and each calf, from its fourth year, does the same. The herd grows by a(n) = a(n−1) + a(n−3): 1, 1, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 13, 19, 28, 41, … The ratio of consecutive terms converges to the supergolden ratio ψ ≈ 1.4655712 — the unique real root of x³ = x² + 1, a cousin of the golden and plastic ratios.
