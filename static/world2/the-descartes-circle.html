THE DESCARTES CIRCLE

four kissing circles bound by one curvature law
1 WHAT IT IS · WHAT IT DOES · FACT OR FICTION
Descartes’ circle theorem binds four mutually tangent (“kissing”) circles by a single law on their curvatures k = 1/r: (k1+k2+k3+k4)² = 2(k1²+k2²+k3²+k4²). Given three tangent circles, the fourth’s curvature is k4 = k1+k2+k3 ± 2√(k1k2+k2k3+k3k1) — two solutions, an inner and an outer kiss. A complex version gives the fourth circle’s center too.
