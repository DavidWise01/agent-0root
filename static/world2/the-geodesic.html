THE GEODESIC

In general relativity gravity is not a force. A free body coasts along the straightest available path — a geodesic — through curved spacetime, tracing the worldline of extremal proper time. Because the path is geometry, not a pull, all masses fall alike: the equivalence principle. Rendered, not quoted — the universality of free fall, the great-circle geodesic, geodesic deviation as tidal gravity, and the null path of light are all computed live below.

THE MODEL

A free particle extremizes proper time τ = ∫ dτ. In flat spacetime the extremal timelike path is the straight, constant-velocity worldline (a maximum of τ — the twin paradox). In curved spacetime the same variational law bends the path: this is gravity.

Free fall obeys a = g, independent of mass or composition — the equivalence principle. On a sphere the geodesic is the great circle; two initially-parallel great circles converge, and that convergence is tidal gravity (geodesic deviation).

THE GRAVEYARD

Gravity is a force pulling masses together. → It is curvature; free bodies feel no force — they follow geodesics.

The bending of starlight is 0.875″ (Newtonian photon). → 1.75″ — twice, because space curves too.

A geodesic always maximises length/time. → It is stationary; timelike geodesics maximise τ, spacelike ones do not, null ones have τ=0.
