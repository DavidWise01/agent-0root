THE MODEL

A geodesic in the Schwarzschild metric is a Kepler ellipse plus a slow rotation of its apse. Per orbit the perihelion advances by

Δφ = 6πGM / ( a(1−e²)c² )

The Newtonian ellipse closes exactly (Bertrand's theorem: only inverse-square and Hooke forces give closed orbits). The apsidal angle for a force F∝rⁿ is π/√(3+n); for n=−2 it is exactly π, so precession is zero. The 1/c² term is the whole story — it vanishes as c→∞.

THE ADVERSARY wall

"An unseen planet Vulcan inside Mercury's orbit perturbs it — no new physics needed." It was never found. The 43″ is not a Newtonian perturbation by any body; it is the metric itself.
"Solar oblateness (J₂) explains the residual." Measured J₂ is far too small — it accounts for <0.1″/century, not 43.
Precise inputs matter: a, e, and the period carry real uncertainty. The prediction is robust only to a stated tolerance — claiming 43.000 is false precision.

THE GRAVEYARD

The Newtonian ellipse precesses on its own. → No. Pure inverse-square gives a closed orbit (Bertrand); apsidal angle = π exactly, precession = 0.

The total observed shift (~5600″/cy) is the GR effect. → Most is coordinate (equinox) precession + planetary tugs; the GR piece is only the 43″ residual.

Drop a constant and it's "close enough." → Drop the 6 and you get ~7″ — see window 6. The coefficient is not decorative.
