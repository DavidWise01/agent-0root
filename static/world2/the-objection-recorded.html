1 WHAT IT IS · WHAT IT DOES · FACT OR FICTION
A panel filed an objection against the artifact it belongs to, and the artifact shipped with the objection still in it. The symmetric fold discards 3,766 of 4,096 dimensions on the grounds that the four arms are alike — and the arms are drawn alike, which is not a measurement.
