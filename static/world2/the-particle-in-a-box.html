THE PARTICLE IN A BOX

Confine a quantum particle between two walls and its energy stops being a dial — it becomes a ladder. Only certain standing waves fit, so only certain energies are allowed: En = n²π²ℏ²/2mL². The lowest rung is above zero — a confined particle can never sit perfectly still. Down the center, the box goes in, the engine solves the wave, the quantized ladder comes out. The blue team builds and defends it; the red team tries to break it.

THE MODEL — walls force a ladder

Inside the well V=0; outside V=∞, so the wave must vanish at both walls: ψ(0)=ψ(L)=0. Only sine waves with a whole number of half-wavelengths fit:

THE ADVERSARY

WALL An infinite wall is a fiction. No real potential is infinite, so no real ψ is exactly zero at a boundary — a finite well leaks (ψ tunnels into the wall), holds only a finite number of bound states, and sits every level below the ideal-box value. The box is a solvable cartoon of a real trap.

It is also 1D, non-relativistic, single-particle, and time-independent — it says nothing about spin, interactions, or a particle you actually watch move. "Exact" here means exact for the idealization, which is why the panel lets you shrink L and watch the failure mode of confinement appear as ever-larger energies.

2

THE GRAVEYARD

"A confined particle can have zero energy." Cut. E₁ = π²ℏ²/2mL² > 0 — the zero-point floor. Confinement forbids rest; the witness verifies E₁ > 0 live.

"The levels are evenly spaced." Cut. That is the harmonic oscillator (En=(n+½)ℏω). The box grows as n²: gaps 3,5,7,… widen. Tamper (window 6) forces the false even spacing and is caught.

"Doubling the box halves the energy." Cut, corrected. E ∝ 1/L² — doubling L cuts every level to a quarter; halving L quadruples it. Verified by the witness.

6

THE TAMPER — break it

The red team's move: replace the n² law with a linear one, En=n·E₁ — the "evenly-spaced" lie from the graveyard. The blue team's witness (window 7) is watching.

Linearize the ladder and the ratios collapse from 4, 9 to 2, 3 — the n²-scaling check fails, the witness recomputes and turns red. Nothing is faked; the attack is real and it is caught.
