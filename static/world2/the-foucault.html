1 WHAT IT IS · WHAT IT DOES · FACT OR FICTION
Paris, 1851: Léon Foucault hangs a 67-meter wire in the Panthéon and the public watches the swing plane creep clockwise, hour by hour — the first direct, dynamical proof that the Earth turns, no telescope required. The law is one sine: the plane precesses at Ω sin(latitude) — a full circle per sidereal day at the pole, 31.8 hours in Paris, never at the equator. In the rotating frame the Coriolis term does the work, and the whole motion has an exact closed form: z(t) = e⁻ⁱΩₓᵗ·(oscillation), rotation stamped on as a complex phase.
