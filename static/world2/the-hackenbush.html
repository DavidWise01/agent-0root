1 WHAT IT IS · WHAT IT DOES · FACT OR FICTION
Blue-Red Hackenbush: pictures made of colored edges standing on the ground. Left may cut blue edges, Right cuts red; anything disconnected from the ground falls; whoever cannot move loses. From this child’s game, John Conway discovered that positions have numerical values — and the numbers are the surreal numbers. A single blue edge is worth +1 (one spare move for Left). Blue with red on top? Exactly ½. Blue-red-red? ¼. These are not metaphors: value arithmetic predicts game outcomes — a sum of positions worth exactly 0 is a second-player win, positive means Left wins regardless of who starts.
