The Model

A homogeneous, isotropic universe (the cosmological principle). Its scale factor a(t) stretches every distance; Einstein's equations collapse to two ODEs.

H² = (ȧ/a)² = (8πG/3)ρ − kc²/a² + Λc²/3

Gravity decelerates (the − sign on density); curvature k ∈ {−1,0,+1}; Λ pushes outward.

The Adversary wall

“Ω is a free dial — pick any density and space stays flat.” Wall: flatness is k=0, which forces ρ=ρc exactly. Off that razor, curvature is non-zero by construction; the balance is not negotiable.
“Expansion needs a force pushing outward.” Wall: no — ȧ is an initial condition. Gravity only sets ä (deceleration). Nothing pushes unless Λ>0.
