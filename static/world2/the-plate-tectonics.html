THE PLATE TECTONICS

The Earth's shell is broken into drifting plates — and the sea floor itself is a tape recorder of the drift. New crust is minted at a mid-ocean ridge and carried outward on both sides; as the planet's magnetic field flips, each fresh band freezes in the polarity of its birth, laying down symmetric magnetic stripes that mirror across the ridge. Read a stripe's distance against the age of the reversal that made it and you read the spreading rate. Down the center, data flows: the reversals go in, the engine spreads the floor, the rate comes out. The blue team builds it; the red team tries to break it.

source Vine, F. J. & Matthews, D. H., "Magnetic Anomalies over Oceanic Ridges," Nature 199, 947–949 (1963) — doi.org/10.1038/199947a0; on Wegener (1912) continental drift. Rendered, not quoted.

Sea floor is made at the ridge axis and moves away symmetrically. A reversal at age t is now frozen into crust that sits at distance = rate × t on both flanks — so the stripe pattern is a mirror image across the ridge (the Vine–Matthews evidence). Older floor lies farther out; the age grows monotonically with distance.

THE LINEAGE — the drifting shell AVAN

Wegener (1912) saw the continents fit like torn paper but had no engine; Vine & Matthews (1963) supplied it — sea-floor spreading, proven by the symmetric magnetic stripes, the rate read straight off the stripe width.

What drives it is buoyancy in the mantle: the same density floating that the-isostasy uses to float a crust on its root, here lifting hot rock at the ridge and pulling cold slabs down at the trench. Each sphere is the next one's premise.

DATA OUT — the rate out ↓

What the machine produces, proven: from stripe distance and reversal age it returns the spreading rate (a stripe 20 km out from a 1-Myr reversal = 2 cm/yr), the symmetric-stripe pattern that is the Vine–Matthews proof, and the hard ceiling — no sea floor older than ~180–200 Myr, because it is recycled at subduction zones while continents endure.

THE ADVERSARY

WALL The stripes prove the floor spreads and hand you the rate — but they do not settle why it moves. Ridge-push vs slab-pull vs whole-mantle convection is still weighted argument (slab-pull now thought dominant). The driving force is AMBER, not LIT.

The model also assumes a constant rate between reversals; real half-rates drift and jump, ridges jump, and asymmetric spreading happens. The tape is a clean recorder of that the floor spread — a noisier witness to exactly how fast at every instant.

2

THE GRAVEYARD

"Wegener's drift was accepted in his lifetime." Cut. Rejected for ~50 years — he had no mechanism; vindicated only by sea-floor spreading in the 1960s.

"The continents plow through the sea floor." Cut. Wegener's mechanism was wrong; whole plates (crust + lithospheric mantle) move together, new floor minted at ridges.

"Only Vine & Matthews saw it." Kept, corrected. Lawrence Morley reached the same idea independently in 1963 and was refereed away — it is the Vine–Matthews–Morley hypothesis.

6

THE TAMPER — break it

The red team's move: stretch the far-side stripes so they no longer mirror the near side — a floor that spread unequally and unrecorded. The blue team's witness (window 7) is watching.

Break the symmetry and the pattern is no longer a spreading record — the witness recomputes, finds the flanks disagree, and turns red. Nothing is faked; the attack is real and it is caught.
