1 WHAT IT IS · WHAT IT DOES · FACT OR FICTION
Graham’s number is so large that the observable universe cannot store its digits — not in atoms, not in Planck volumes. And yet its final digits are perfectly knowable: Graham’s number is a tower of 3-exponentials, and modulo 10ᵏ every sufficiently tall tower of 3s stabilizes — the last k digits stop changing as the tower grows.
