THE AZTEC

the frozen diamond
1 WHAT IT IS · WHAT IT DOES · FACT OR FICTION
Stack rows of 2, 4, 6, … squares into a diamond — the Aztec diamond AD(n). Tile it with dominoes. How many ways? Exactly 2^(n(n+1)/2) — a formula so clean that Elkies, Kuperberg, Larsen and Propp gave it four different proofs in one 1992 paper. And inside a RANDOM tiling hides the arctic circle theorem (Jockusch–Propp–Shor): outside the inscribed circle the dominoes freeze into brickwork; all the disorder lives inside the circle. Order at the corners is not designed — it is forced by counting.
