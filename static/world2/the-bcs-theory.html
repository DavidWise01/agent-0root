THE BCS THEORY

Electrons repel — yet just below Tc they quietly pair through the lattice and condense into one frictionless quantum state. The Fermi sea is unstable to any attraction; a gap 2Δ opens, and the critical temperature is exponentially small in the coupling. Rendered, not quoted.

source Bardeen, Cooper & Schrieffer, “Theory of Superconductivity,” Phys. Rev. 108, 1175 (1957) — doi:10.1103/PhysRev.108.1175

3The Model

Two electrons added above a filled Fermi sea, with a weak phonon-mediated attraction acting only within a Debye shell ±ħωD of EF.

Cooper binding: Eb = 2ħωD·e−2/N₀V — nonzero for any N₀V>0.

Gap & Tc: Δ(0)=2ħωDe−1/N₀V, kBTc=1.134ħωDe−1/N₀V.

5The Lineage

Cooper pairs from a phonon attraction, a gap 2Δ=3.53 kBTc, an isotope effect Tc∝M−½.

This is the microscopic engine behind the-meissner-effect — the condensate that expels B is exactly this paired ground state. Neighbour sphere in THE FOLD.

Red Team · attacks & breaks
1The Adversary WALL
“Like charges repel — a bound electron pair is impossible.” True in vacuum. The wall is the filled Fermi sea: it supplies a constant density of states so that even an arbitrarily weak attraction produces a bound state. The instability is in the medium, not the two electrons alone.
2The Graveyard

Superconductivity is a perfect classical conductor (σ→∞ only).

→ It is a distinct thermodynamic phase that expels field (Meissner); a perfect conductor would merely freeze flux in.

The gap is a fixed material constant.

→ Δ(T) collapses to 0 at Tc; only the ratio 2Δ(0)/kBTc≈3.53 is universal.

Isotope mass is irrelevant to an electronic effect.

→ Tc∝M−½ — the phonon fingerprint that proved the lattice mediates the pairing.

6The Tamper

Planted void: force the gap to zero (2Δ=0), so pairing yields no gap and the 3.53 ratio dies. The Witness (7) catches it live.
