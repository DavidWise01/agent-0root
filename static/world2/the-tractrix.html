THE TRACTRIX

the leash with constant length
1 WHAT IT IS · WHAT IT DOES · FACT OR FICTION
Drag a reluctant dog on a taut leash while you walk a straight line: the dog traces the tractrix (Huygens named it, 1692) — the curve whose defining property is that the tangent segment from any point to the axis has constant length: the leash. Its closed form is a(t−tanh t), a·sech t. Revolve it and you get Beltrami’s pseudosphere (1868): an infinite trumpet with constant Gaussian curvature −1 — the first concrete home of non-Euclidean geometry, where hyperbolic axioms become facts about a surface you can hold. And the infinite trumpet’s total area is exactly 4πa² — the same as a sphere’s.
