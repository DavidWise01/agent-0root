THE MODEL

Light follows a null geodesic through spacetime curved by mass M. For a ray with impact parameter b (closest approach), General Relativity gives a deflection:

α = 4GM / (b·c²)

Grazing the Sun's limb (b = R☉) this is 1.75″. The Einstein-ring radius for a source dead-behind the lens:

θE = √(4GM·Dls / (c²·Dl·Ds))

Both scale with mass: α∝M, θE∝√M. Inverse in b — closer grazing bends more.

THE GRAVEYARD

"Light has no mass, so gravity cannot touch it."
→ Gravity acts on energy/momentum and on the path itself; massless light still follows the curved geodesic.

"The 1919 error bars were too big to prove anything."
→ Sobral+Príncipe favoured 1.75″ over 0.875″ and over zero; later radio/VLBI pinned it to <0.02%.

"A lens magnifies but conserves surface brightness, so it adds no light."
→ True for brightness, but it enlarges solid angle — total flux is magnified; that is how lensing reveals faint high-z sources.
