the mapping: 2 × 27 = 54 = 52 cards + 2 jokers. Color is the high bit (red page / black page), each page 26 cards + 1 joker = 27 = 3 trits, so the address is 1 color-bit + 3 trits. Number cards 2–10 (36) are reversible → fire freely; A/J/Q/K (16) are the power cards → gate + witness + confirm; the 2 jokers are special (HALT/wild). Honest note: the deck is stored in the dual-27 lattice — color maps cleanly to the bit, but suit×rank (4×13) doesn't factor into ternary (13 isn't a power of 3), so it's a container, not an isomorphism.
