THE NONTRANSITIVE DICE

dice with no best
1 WHAT IT IS · WHAT IT DOES · FACT OR FICTION
Four dice, honest weights, strange faces: A = [4,4,4,4,0,0], B = [3,3,3,3,3,3], C = [6,6,2,2,2,2], D = [5,5,5,1,1,1]. A beats B, B beats C, C beats D — and D beats A, every arrow at exactly 2/3. These are Efron’s nontransitive dice: preference cycles in physical form, rock-paper-scissors smuggled into cubes. There is no best die — whichever your opponent picks, one of the remaining three beats it two times in three. Warren Buffett famously offered Bill Gates first pick of a nontransitive set; Gates examined the dice and insisted Buffett choose first.

LIT verified live by complete enumeration: all 36 outcomes for each adjacent pair — 24/36 = 2/3 exactly, four times around the cycle; and the no-best-die claim checked over the full beat graph (window.__nontransitivedice).
