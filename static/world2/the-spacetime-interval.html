THE SPACETIME INTERVAL

Two observers flying past each other disagree on time and disagree on distance — but they agree on one number: s² = (cΔt)² − Δx². It is the same in every inertial frame, and its sign is the law of cause: it splits the future you can reach from the elsewhere you never can. Down the center the two events go in, the metric decides, the invariant comes out. The blue team builds and defends it; the red team tries to break it.

THE ADVERSARY

WALL The invariant interval is a statement about flat, inertial spacetime. In a gravitational field only the infinitesimal line element ds² = gμνdxμdxν is invariant; the global s² = t² − x² has no meaning on a curved manifold. Minkowski's metric is the tangent-space limit, not the whole of General Relativity.

The signature itself is a convention: (+,−,−,−) here flips to (−,+,+,+) in many texts, and s² changes sign with it — only the classification is physical, not the bare number. Accelerated (non-inertial) frames need the full apparatus; the interval is the first true statement, not the last.

2

THE GRAVEYARD

"Moving clocks just run slow — it's an illusion of measurement." Cut. Proper time τ=s/c is frame-independent and physical; the twin who accelerates really is younger. The interval is what makes that objective.

"Nothing about the past and future is absolute in relativity." Cut. For timelike/lightlike pairs the time order is absolute — causality is preserved in every frame. Only spacelike pairs can be reordered.

"The interval is just the Pythagorean distance in 4D." Kept, corrected. It has the form of a metric but the crucial minus sign — time enters with the opposite sign to space. That one sign is the whole of causality.

6

THE TAMPER — break it

The red team's move: swap the metric's minus for a plus — make spacetime Euclidean, s² = t² + x². Now boosts no longer preserve it and the light cone no longer separates the regions. The blue team's witness (window 7) is watching.

Flip the sign and the interval stops being invariant; the witness re-boosts the fixed events, finds the number changing frame-to-frame, and turns red. Nothing is faked; the attack is real and it is caught.
