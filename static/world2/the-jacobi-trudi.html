THE JACOBI-TRUDI

a Schur polynomial as a determinant of complete symmetrics
1 WHAT IT IS · WHAT IT DOES · FACT OR FICTION
The Jacobi–Trudi identity writes a Schur polynomial — the fundamental building block of symmetric-function theory — as a determinant. The Schur polynomial sλ is defined combinatorially as a sum over all semistandard Young tableaux of shape λ (fillings that weakly increase along rows and strictly increase down columns). Jacobi and Trudi proved it also equals a clean determinant of complete homogeneous symmetric polynomials: sλ = det(hλi-i+j). A messy sum over combinatorial objects becomes one determinant of simple pieces — the bridge that connects representation theory, symmetric functions, and algebraic combinatorics.

LIT verified live: for several partitions λ and random values of the variables, the Jacobi–Trudi determinant det(hλi-i+j) equals the direct sum over all semistandard Young tableaux of shape λ, to floating precision (window.__jacobitrudi). FIG no framing; the determinant of complete-homogeneous polynomials and the tableau sum are computed by different routes and agree.
