THE 32.

2⁵ = 32 is the vertex count of a 5-dimensional hypercube — a penteract. Each vertex is a 5-bit address (00000…11111); each edge flips exactly one bit. 32 vertices, 80 edges, folded from five dimensions onto the page and turning. Where the 512 rung was a 3-cube in space, the 32 is a 5-cube in address.
